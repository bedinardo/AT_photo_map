Welcome to the Appalachian Trail!

The Appalachian Trail spans nearly 2,200 miles, running across 13 states and over a hundred peaks from Georgia to Maine. On March 3rd 2021, Bryce DiNardo (myself) and Kasey Drake set out on a semester off of a lifetime as they hiked the AT Northbound for a total of 162 days, averaging 18.5 miles a day when hiking.
Explore their thru-hiking journey through geotagged photos of hikers, mountaintops, trail signs, and views they captured along the way.
Visit a few of their favorite and most iconic places by clicking "Explore the Appalachian Trail" button at the top of the screen multiple times.
